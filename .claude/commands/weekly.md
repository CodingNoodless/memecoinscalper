---
description: Weekly review - prune the pipeline, check progress against the money targets
---
1. Scoreboard and registry: how many ideas were killed / passed this week, average days to a verdict.
2. Progress vs targets ($1k by Nov 15, $10k by Dec 31): best forward edge × size × trades/day = projected $/day.
3. Prune: kill anything stuck in CONTINUE for > 7 days without movement toward n = 100; close stale programs.
4. Pick next week's ≤ 3 bets by expected value per hour. Record it in a `Reviews/` note and Decisions.
